## 2. The 90-second screen video

The most useful thing you can make. It goes in messages, in ads and on the site.
Record your phone screen and talk over it. One take is fine.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A trainer's page opening | "This is a trainer's page on Train.futbol. Their name, their colors." |
| 0:10 | Picking sessions for a player's week | "After a session, the trainer picks two or three short sessions for the week." |
| 0:25 | The player's side: today's session | "The player opens the link. Today's session is there." |
| 0:35 | A drill with the video playing, then checked off | "Each drill has the trainer's video and the reps. They check it off when it's done." |
| 0:55 | The trainer's player list | "The trainer sees every player's week on one line: on track, one behind, not seen since Tuesday." |
| 1:10 | Tapping the reminder | "One tap sends a reminder." |
| 1:20 | train.futbol/trainers | "Ten dollars a player a month, and the first week is free. I set up your page with you. train.futbol." |

Use your own drills or a trainer's in the recording, not the licensed library.

## 3. A 15-minute demo

**Minutes 0 to 3: ask, don't show.**

- How many players do you train? Private, groups, or both?
- What do you give them to do between sessions? How do you send it?
- How do you know if they did it?

Write down their words. You'll use them in the last five minutes.

**Minutes 3 to 8: the player's side.** Hand them your phone with a session open. Let
them tap through a drill and check it off. Say little.

**Minutes 8 to 12: the trainer's side.** Show the player list, then build a week for
one player in front of them. Use what they told you: "You said you send YouTube links
by text. This is that same video, here."

**Minutes 12 to 15: the ask.**

- "What would you want in your first three sessions?"
- "I'll set your page up myself. It's $10 a player a month, or $5 a player if you'd
  rather charge your players your own way. Every player's first 7 days are free."
- "Which would suit you?" If they ask how it's paid: at $10 the player's family pays
  by card and you pay nothing; at $5 you pay for your players and charge them however
  you like. Write down the answer; it's the most useful thing you'll learn.
- "Can we do the setup on [day]? I need 20 minutes and your drill list."

Leave with a date, not "I'll think about it".

## 4. What trainers will ask

**"I don't have time to set this up."**
You don't have to. Send me a voice memo of three sessions you already run and I'll
build them. It takes you 20 minutes.

**"My players won't do it."**
Some won't. Now you'll know which ones, and so will their parents. The ones who do will
improve faster, and that's what parents notice.

**"I already send videos by text / WhatsApp."**
That works for sending. It doesn't tell you who did the work, and the drills get buried
in the thread. This keeps the week in one place and shows you who finished.

**"I don't have videos of my drills."**
Film them on your phone, held sideways, next time you're on the field. Ten drills
takes twenty minutes. A session works without video too.

**"Is it another app parents have to download?"**
No. It opens from a link and works like an app.

**"Who owns my drills and my players?"**
You do. Your page has your name on it. Other trainers can't see your drills or your
players, and neither can your players' clubs.

**"$10 a player adds up."**
At $10 it's the player's family who pays, by card, not you. If you'd rather it came
from you, it's $5 a player and you charge your players however you like.

**"Will parents pay $10 a month?"**
A family whose player trains with you weekly already pays you $320 to $500 a month.
This is $10 on top, for a plan that makes those sessions count. The first 7 days are
free, so they see their player using it before they pay, and they can cancel any time.

**"I don't want my clients charged by someone else."**
Then take the $5 price: $5 a player a month, billed to you, and your families pay
nothing to us. You can fold it into your package price.

**"Won't they book fewer sessions if they can train at home?"**
The drills are homework for your session, not a replacement for it. Players who
practise see progress, and parents who see progress keep booking. Tell families the
at-home sessions come with regular training, not instead of it.

**"What about kids' privacy?"**
A parent signs in with their own email for younger players. Players see only their own
training. There are no ads and no public profiles. [Confirm this against the privacy
page once it has been reviewed.]

**"Do I get a share of what families pay?"**
Not at the moment. What you gain is players who practise, improve and keep booking.

**"Can players pay me through it?"**
No. Families pay Train.futbol for the at-home plan. You keep charging for your
sessions the way you do now.

**"I coach a club team. Can I use it?"**
It's built for private trainers right now. Take their details and come back to them.

## 5. Setting up a new trainer

Aim for the trainer's players to have a session to do within 48 hours of the yes.

- [ ] Approve their request and send the claim link
- [ ] Get from them: page name and address, colors, three or four sessions, video links
- [ ] Agree who pays: families ($10 a month after the free days) or the trainer ($5 a
      player)
- [ ] Build their drills and sessions for them
- [ ] 20-minute call: they add two players themselves, build one week, and see the
      player list
- [ ] Give them the parent message below and their invite link
- [ ] Day 3: check how many players joined. If under half, ask what's in the way.
- [ ] Day 7: send them a screenshot of their own player list with a short note
- [ ] Day 14: the check-in email in `trainer-emails.md`
- [ ] When the families' free days end: check how many subscribed, and ask the
      trainer about the ones who didn't

## 6. The message a trainer sends to parents

Getting players on is where most trainers stall, so give them the words.

> Hi [Parent],
>
> I'm starting something new for [Player]. After each session I'll set two or three
> short sessions for the week, 15 to 30 minutes each, with videos of the drills. They
> do them at home and check them off, and I can see what they've done before I see
> them next.
>
> Here's the link to join: [invite link]
>
> There's no app to download. It's free for the first 7 days. After that it's $10 a
> month, paid by card to Train.futbol, and you can cancel any time.
>
> For players under 13, please sign in with your own email. Tell me which days
> [Player] has practice and games and the sessions will fit around them.
>
> [Trainer name]

For a founding trainer, change "7 days" to "3 months". If the trainer is covering
it, replace the two price sentences with "It's included in your training."

## 7. One-page summary

For printing, or pasting into a message when someone asks "what is it?"

> **Train.futbol: one session a week, training all week.**
>
> For private soccer trainers. You see each player about once a week. Train.futbol is
> for the other six days.
>
> **How it works**
> 1. After your session, pick two or three short sessions for the player's week.
> 2. They train at home, with your videos, 15 to 30 minutes at a time.
> 3. You see who did it, and send a reminder in one tap to who didn't.
>
> **What you get**
> - Your own page at yourname.train.futbol, with your name and colors
> - Sessions built from the drills you already teach
> - Each player's week on one line, before you see them next
> - Groups, individual players, or both
> - Nothing for players to download
>
> **Price:** $10 a player a month, or $5 a player if you'd rather charge your players
> your own way. Every player's first 7 days are free. No setup fee, no contract.
> Founding trainers: your first 3 months free.
>
> **Start:** train.futbol/start · [your email]

## 8. Instagram: the first two weeks

Three posts a week. Each one is useful to a trainer even if they never sign up.

| # | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You see each player once a week. What happens on the other six days?" | The week-strip graphic from the deck |
| 2 | The 90-second screen video, cut to 30 seconds | Reel |
| 3 | "Three at-home sessions any player can do in 15 minutes" (your own drills) | Carousel |
| 4 | How a trainer builds a player's week in one minute | Screen recording |
| 5 | "What do you send players home with?" Ask trainers to answer | Question post |
| 6 | Trainer zero's page, and one line from them | Photo and quote |

After that, repeat the pattern: one post about the problem, one showing the product,
one that features a trainer. Tag every trainer you feature.

## 10. Asking for a testimonial

Ask at two weeks, when a trainer has seen their player list fill in. Record it on
their phone at the field, 20 to 30 seconds.

Questions that get usable answers:

1. What did you do about homework before this?
2. What's different now?
3. What would you say to another trainer thinking about it?

Get their written OK to use their name, their words and the video on the site and in
ads. If a player appears in the video, get the parent's written OK too.
