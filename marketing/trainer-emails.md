# Trainer outreach: emails and DMs

Everything here is written to one person: an independent private soccer trainer who
sees each player about once a week. Fill in the `[brackets]` before sending. Keep the
first line personal every time; it is what gets the email read.

Before you send cold email: add your mailing address and a one-line way to opt out
("Reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again") to your signature. US law (CAN-SPAM)
asks for both on commercial email.

---

## 1. Cold email (the main one)

**Subject options** (pick one, test two):

- What do your players do between sessions?
- Homework for [Academy name] players
- Quick question, [First name]

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

[One true line about them: "I saw your first-touch series on Instagram" / "My neighbor's
daughter trains with you on Thursdays".]

I'm a soccer parent, and I built something for private trainers that I'd like your
opinion on. [Check this line is true for you, or replace it with your own reason.]

You see each player about once a week. Train.futbol covers the other six days. You
pick two or three short sessions from your own drills, your players do them at home
with your videos, and you see who did them before the next session.

Each trainer gets their own page (yourname.train.futbol) with their name and colors.
Players join from a link, with no app to download.

It's $10 a player a month, or $5 a player if you'd rather charge your players your
own way. I'm setting up the first 3 trainers myself, and their first 3 months are
free. Could I show you in 15 minutes this week? Or look at what a player sees here:
demo.train.futbol

Thanks,
Christian
train.futbol

## 5. Instagram or text message (short)

**First message:**

Hi [First name], I like the [specific drill or post] you put up. Quick question: do you
give your players anything to do at home between sessions, and do you know if they
do it?

**If they answer** (most trainers say "I send videos, no idea if they do them"):

That's what I built Train.futbol for. You pick drills for their week, they do them at
home with your videos, and you see who did them. Your own page, your name on it.
I'm setting up the first few trainers myself, free for 3 months. Can I send you a
90-second video of how it works?

**Then send:** the screen recording (see `launch-kit.md`) and train.futbol/trainers.

## 6. After they say yes

**Subject:** Your Train.futbol page: what I need from you

Hi [First name],

Glad you're in. To set up your page I need five things, and a reply to this email is
fine:

1. The name you want on your page and the address you'd like (for example
   `[name].train.futbol`).
2. Three or four sessions you already run. A voice memo or a list of drills is enough.
3. Links to any drill videos you have. YouTube works. If you have none, film them on
   your phone held sideways and send them over; I'll put them up for you.
4. Roughly how many players you'd start with.
5. Which price suits you: $10 a player a month, or $5 a player if you'd rather
   charge your players your own way. I'll explain how each is paid when we talk.

I'll have your page ready within [two days]. Then we spend 20 minutes together so you
can see your players' week and send them the link.

Christian

## 7. Two weeks in: the check-in that gets you a testimonial

**Subject:** How's it going with your players?

Hi [First name],

You've had [N] players on Train.futbol for two weeks and they've finished [N]
sessions between them. Three questions, short answers are fine:

1. What's the most useful thing about it so far?
2. What's annoying or missing?
3. Have you noticed anything different in your sessions?

If you're happy with it, could I quote you on the website? And is there another
trainer you'd tell about it? I'll set them up the same way I did for you.

Christian
